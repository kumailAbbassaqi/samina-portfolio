[P] 1. Grounding in Vision
[P] 1.1 Campaign Vision
[P] Our campaign vision is to build a culture of accountability in the education system and society. We believe that accountability is the foundation of quality education: teachers must be present and prepared, schools must be responsive, and communities must actively participate.
[P]  Long-Term Change We Want to Build
[P] Our vision connects to our long-term change — a society where education is a shared responsibility, where all this factors are present:
[P] Accountable Schools
[P] Principals leading with responsibility to ensure discipline, regular classes, and teacher performance.
[P] Regular monitoring by DDO, Education Department, and SMCs to keep schools transparent and effective.
[P] Motivated Teachers & Active Learning
[P] Teachers supported with training, recognition, and guidance.
[P] Activity-based and creative teaching methods to make learning enjoyable and effective.
[P] Empowered Parents & Strong SMCs
[P] Parents actively engaged through PTMs and decision-making.
[P] Restructured, functional SMCs leading community accountability and school improvement.
[P] Interested & Confident Students
[P] Students developing a genuine love for studies through interactive and engaging classes.
[P] Equal encouragement for boys and girls to participate, ask questions, and excel.
[P] Equal Access for Girls
[P] Girls supported and encouraged to continue education at every level.
[P] Community and religious leaders actively advocating for girls’ education.

[P] 1.2 Purpose Check: Why the Saheli Circle?
[P] Why are you running a Saheli circle as part of your campaign?
[P] We are running a Saheli Circle as part of our campaign because almost all students in Saltoro do not ask questions in class, show little interest in their studies, and often lack confidence. The Saheli Circle provides a safe and supportive space where students can share their thoughts, learn to ask questions, build self-confidence, and develop essential life skills, which are the right of every student. In addition, they will learn leadership, decision-making, communication, and negotiation skills, enabling them to actively participate in school and community life. By encouraging open discussion and peer support, the Saheli Circle helps students become active learners, take greater interest in education, and prepare for life beyond school.
[P] What role is it playing in your campaign strategy -Awareness , base building, narrative shift , organizing?
[P] The Saheli Circle is playing a key role in awareness, base building, and organizing in our campaign strategy. By bringing together all girls students in Saltoro, it helps raise awareness about their rights, the importance of education, and the campaign itself. The girls also share this awareness with their parents, creating a stronger community understanding. Its main purpose is to build their interest in studies and prepare them to actively question and hold teachers accountable. Through peer support and skill-building activities, the Circle organizes girls around shared issues like education participation, leadership, and life skills, strengthening their collective voice and fostering active participation in school and community decisions.
[P]
[P] How are you using the circle to move others to act not just attend?
[P]
[P]
[P] The Saheli Circle ensures that girls do not just attend sessions but actively participate in the campaign. Through discussions, skill-building activities, and peer support, they develop confidence, leadership, communication, and decision-making skills. Equipped with these skills, girls encourage their peers to engage in school activities, ask questions, and hold teachers accountable. They also share their learning with parents, raising awareness and motivating action across the community. In this way, the Circle transforms passive attendance into active participation, expanding the campaign’s impact beyond the Circle itself.
